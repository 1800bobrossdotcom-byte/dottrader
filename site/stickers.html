Print at home

Set how many of each above, choose your paper, and print onto sticker paper (or plain paper and glue). Print at 100% / actual size — not “fit to page” — so they come out the right size.

Order printed stickers

  1. Die-cut (cut to shape): upload the PNG to Sticker Mule, StickerApp or any die-cut printer and pick 3″. The white border is already there and the background is transparent, so they cut around the shape automatically.
  2. Square: upload the PDF and pick 3″ × 3″. It includes the ⅛″ bleed printers trim off.
  3. Choose matte for “What would you trade for this?” so people can write on it.
