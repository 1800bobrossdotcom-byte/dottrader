How it works

Three agents propose, one agent vetoes, and everything is scored in DOT rather than dollars.

01 — ACCUMULATE

ETH becomes inventory.

Trading wallets start with ETH only. A daily budget converts it into $DOT, buying harder into dips and after burns.

02 — GRID & MEAN-REVERSION

Sell strength, buy it back lower.

Thin slices are sold as DOT/ETH climbs ~7% levels and repurchased at least 3% cheaper. The pool's ~1.1% swap fee is priced into every level.

03 — RISK & VAULT

Nothing leaves the core.

The core stack is never sold, selling halts if the day is down 3% in DOT, and round-trip profit is swept to the vault wallet.
