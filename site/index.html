Three steps, no account to fill in

The whole app is one loop. If it takes longer than a minute to post something, it's broken.

01 — POST

Say what you have.

A title, a line about condition, and what you'd want back. "Open to offers" is a fine answer.

02 — OFFER

Someone proposes a swap.

They say what they'd give. You see their dots before you decide, so you know who you're dealing with.

03 — TRADE

Accept, meet, vouch.

Accepting takes the item off the board. When it's in hand, you each vouch — and you each earn dots.

Dots are earned, never bought

A barter board runs on whether you can trust a stranger. Dots are the only answer to that, so they're the one thing the app guards.

+2for every completed trade, both sides
+1when your trade partner vouches afterwards
+1for filling in your profile
0for posting, browsing, or anything you can do alone

Nobody can award themselves one

Dots aren't stored as a number anyone can write. They're counted from the trade record itself — every dot traces back to a swap somebody else accepted, or a vouch somebody else left. There's nothing to edit and nothing to buy.
