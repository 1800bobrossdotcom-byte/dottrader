Three ways to swap

Sign up with your email, list what you have — a card, a console, an NFT — and trade it for whatever you'd rather have.

NFT ⇄ NFT · ANY CHAIN

Trade NFTs across chains.

Ethereum for Base, Zora for Polygon. On the same chain it's one transaction — both move or neither does. Across chains, the board checks on chain that each side still holds theirs.

THING ⇄ NFT

Barter physical for digital.

A sealed booster box for a piece of art you love. The NFT is checked on chain, the box gets a ship-by date and tracking, and a no-show is on the record. Digital-for-physical, finally without the leap of faith.

THING ⇄ THING

Cards for consoles, by post or meeting up locally. Tracking both ways, four days to send, and every trader's record of trades and no-shows on their card.

Three steps, no account to fill in

The whole app is one loop. If it takes longer than a minute to post something, it's broken.

01 — POST

Say what you have.

A title, a line about condition, and what you'd want back. "Open to offers" is a fine answer.

02 — OFFER

Someone proposes a swap.

They say what they'd give. You see their dots before you decide, so you know who you're dealing with.

03 — TRADE

Accept, meet, vouch.

Accepting takes the item off the board. When it's in hand, you each vouch — and you each earn dots.

Built so neither trader has to just hope

The hard part of swapping with a stranger is sending first. Every step of a trade on the board is designed around that.

Trust you can read

Every trader shows their dots, how many trades they've finished, how many were verified, and any no-shows — before you agree to anything.

A deadline, and a record

Once you agree, both sides have four days to send. Mark yours sent with a tracking number. If the other side never sends, close it as a no-show — it costs them dots, and everyone can see it.

Talk privately

Each trade has its own message thread that only the two of you can read. Arrange a meetup or a postage plan without posting anything public.

Near, not exact

"Near me" uses a location rounded to about a kilometre, enforced by the database. Nobody sees your address unless you send it.

NFTs that can't be half-traded

When both sides are NFTs on the same chain, the swap is one transaction: both move, or neither does. Nobody sends first. Listings are checked on chain, so you know it's still held.

No money in the middle

Trades are item for item. No money moves through the board and no card numbers are stored here, so there's nothing in the middle to lose.

Dots are earned, never bought

A barter board runs on whether you can trust a stranger. Dots are the only answer to that, so they're the one thing the app guards.

Nobody can award themselves one

Dots aren't stored as a number anyone can write. They're counted from the trade record itself — every dot traces back to a swap somebody else accepted, or a vouch somebody else left. Trading back and forth with the same person stops counting after the second time, so two friends can't manufacture a reputation. There's nothing to edit and nothing to buy.
